# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Open an issue before beginning a substantial feature so maintainers can confirm scope and design.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it and agree that it is provided under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Do not include customer data, credentials, confidential information, third-party source code, or content without the required rights.

Sign commits in accordance with the [Developer Certificate of Origin](DCO.md).
